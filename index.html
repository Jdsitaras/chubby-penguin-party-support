Player support

How can we help?

Found a bug, have a question, or need help with a purchase? Contact John, the game's developer.

Email support

You can also write directly to jdsitaras@gmail.com.

Reporting a problem

A few details will help us understand what happened:

  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.
  • The app version, if you know it.
  • What happened and what you were doing just before it.
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the problem.

Questions about a purchase

Tell us which item or gem pack you purchased, roughly when you purchased it, and what went wrong. For an Apple refund request, visit Apple's Report a Problem website.

Please do not send passwords, verification codes, payment-card details, or other sensitive information. Check screenshots for personal information before sharing them.
