Reporting a problem

A few details will help us understand what happened:

  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.
  • The app version, if you know it.
  • What happened just before the problem.
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the problem.

Questions about a purchase

Tell us which item or gem pack you purchased, roughly when you purchased it, and what went wrong.

For an Apple refund request, visit Apple's Report a Problem website.

Please do not send passwords, verification codes, or payment-card details. Check screenshots for personal information before sharing them.
