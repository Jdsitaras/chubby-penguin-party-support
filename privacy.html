Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Who is responsible

This policy covers John Sitaras’s mobile game currently listed as Chubby Penguin Party (app identifier com.jdsitaras.penguinslide.public). This policy explains how information is handled when you play the game, use its Apple services, visit our support website, or contact support. For privacy questions or requests, email jdsitaras@gmail.com.

Information saved on your device

The game saves progress, personal results, settings, tutorial progress, gem balances, unlocked items, and equipped cosmetics on your device. It also keeps purchase-delivery records so that the same purchase is not credited twice. The game generates random identifiers to distinguish saved profiles and devices when reconciling saves. These are not advertising identifiers.

Cloud saves

When the game's iCloud save service is available, the game sends a copy of your save to Apple's CloudKit service in the private database associated with your iCloud account. The save includes your wallet and item ownership, purchase transaction identifiers and product quantities, personal run results, selected character, game preferences, tutorial progress, and save-reconciliation identifiers. Run results include scores, distances, dates, and challenge information. They are not a video or a frame-by-frame recording of your play.

This information is used to save and recover progress, reconcile supported saves across devices, and prevent duplicate delivery of purchases. The private save is not displayed to other players. We do not operate a separate gameplay database outside Apple for these saves. Apple's services also process information under Apple's privacy terms: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Game Center and leaderboards

If you use Game Center, the game uses your Game Center player identifier and display name to connect you to Apple's leaderboard service. It submits eligible scores and distances and reads leaderboard entries to show rankings. Your Game Center name and submitted results may be visible to other players on leaderboards. You can manage your Game Center profile and privacy preferences in Apple's settings. Signing out does not necessarily delete previously submitted results. See https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/game-center/.

Purchases

Apple processes payments for in-app purchases. We do not receive your payment-card number or Apple Account password. The app processes Apple's verified transaction information, including product and transaction identifiers, purchased quantities, and a save-profile token, to deliver gems and prevent duplicate credits. Delivery records and balances are included in your private cloud save when cloud saving is available. Apple's transaction history and our saved gem balance are different: restoring a balance does not mean crediting every old purchase again.

Support

If you email support, we receive your email address, the name associated with your email, the message, and any attachments you choose to send. This may include device information or screenshots. We use it to answer your request, investigate a problem, and resolve purchase or privacy questions. Email is handled through Gmail. Please send only information needed for your request, and never passwords, verification codes, or payment-card details. Google's privacy policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Diagnostics, advertising, and recordings

The public release does not include the custom development run recorder, advertising SDKs, or a separate behavioral analytics service. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking.

Apple may make crash or usage reports available according to your Apple sharing choices and Apple's policies. Reports we receive are used to diagnose crashes and improve reliability. This is separate from recording your runs ourselves.

Support website

Our support site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics scripts, advertising trackers, cookies, embedded third-party fonts, or a contact form. GitHub processes visitor information, including IP addresses for security, under its own policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Clicking an email link opens your email application; sending the message is your choice. Links to Apple or other providers take you to their services and policies.

Sharing and protection

Information is used by the services described above to provide the game, payments, saves, rankings, website hosting, and support. Public leaderboard information is visible as explained above. We may also disclose information when legally required or necessary to address fraud, security incidents, or legal claims. We do not share support messages or private saves for advertising.

We use appropriate access controls and the protections provided by our service providers. No storage or transmission system is completely secure. Providers may process information in the United States or other countries where they operate. Their international-transfer protections are described in their privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until it is removed from the device. Device backups and cloud saves may retain separate copies. Deleting the app does not by itself guarantee deletion of iCloud saves, submitted leaderboards, or Apple's payment records.

Cloud progress is retained to provide saving and recovery. To remove it, first close and remove the game from devices that hold that save, then use Apple’s iCloud storage controls to remove the game’s data. A remaining local copy may sync back to iCloud. Device backups may also contain a separate copy. Contact support if you need help identifying the relevant app data. This process does not delete Apple purchase records or previously submitted Game Center scores. Purchase-delivery records are retained with the save while needed to maintain balances and prevent duplicate credits. Apple retains its own payment records according to its policies and legal obligations.

Support messages are kept while needed to respond to the request, resolve related issues, and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of information we control by contacting us.

For information we control, you can request access, correction, or deletion by emailing jdsitaras@gmail.com. We may need limited information to verify that a request concerns your data. We will explain any information we must retain and why. We cannot delete your Apple Account or bypass Apple's controls. For data controlled by Apple, use Apple's privacy tools or contact Apple.

Your choices and rights

You can choose whether to send a support message and manage Apple service and sharing choices through your device settings. Turning off a service can prevent features such as cloud recovery or leaderboards from working. Disabling sharing does not necessarily erase information previously stored.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process information necessary to provide requested game services and purchases to perform that service; support and reliability information for our legitimate interests in answering requests and maintaining the game; and legally required records to comply with our obligations. Optional future collection will use consent where required.

Children and families

The game is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children through support. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and remove it as required. Parents can use Apple’s parental controls for purchases and Game Center.

Changes

We will update this policy when our practices change and state the effective date. If we later add optional run analytics or advertising, we will update disclosures and obtain any required permission before the new collection begins.
